Penguin Winter Lined Gloves Penguin EN 511 and A2 on select styles

Built for winter and freezer work

Cold is its own hazard. Hands stiffen and lose grip long before the rest of the body complains, and a glove that solves warmth by adding bulk trades one problem for another. Penguin runs from a light three-season knit up to a waterproof insulated build, all on the same pre-curved fit.

Key features of Penguin gloves

Grip that survives wet and cold

Every style is latex coated at the palm, from crinkled and foam latex for grip and wear resistance up to a closed-cell dip with a sandy latex overlay that repels water on the wettest work.

Insulation in the liner, not in bulk

Warmth comes from the knit itself: a heavyweight napped terry with an acrylic liner, or a brushed 10-gauge insulated acrylic under a 13-gauge nylon shell. Pre-curved fingers keep the hand relaxed through a full shift.

Seen in low light

Hi-vis orange and yellow knits run through the line, and the 3396 hi-vis colors are built from ANSI 107 approved materials for visibility on short winter days.
