The materials

The protection is knit into the product

Majestic engineers the materials before it builds the glove. Korplex®, Armor Skin™ and Alycore™ are knit and cut directly into the products above, integrating some of the strongest fibers on earth to meet ANSI/ISEA standards while keeping workers comfortable and in control.

Korplex®

An engineered yarn created by blending some of the strongest fibers available. Knit into hand and arm protection, it keeps tactility, flexibility and a comfortable stretch.

  • Blended high-strength fibers

    Exceptional cut resistance without the stiffness that usually comes with it.

  • Protection in the knit

    Integrated directly into the knit, so durability and protection are structural, not a coating.

  • Light and agile

    A seamless, stretchy knit that allows precision handling and confident grip all day.

Type Engineered yarn
Tested to ANSI/ISEA 105-2024
Found in Cut-Less®

Armor Skin™

Superior wear and abrasion resistance that outlasts traditional leather, in a lighter, more flexible alternative that does not sacrifice strength.

  • Outperforms leather

    More abrasion resistance at a lighter weight, with more flexibility and no loss of strength.

  • Built for long shifts

    Engineered for an exceptional fit and feel, with comfort and dexterity hour after hour.

  • Takes insulation or TPR

    Pairs with cold-weather lining or impact-resistant TPR backing over knuckles and fingers.

Type Synthetic leather
Tested to ANSI/ISEA 105-2024
Found in Knucklehead X10®

Alycore™

A patented technology made from ultra-thin threads of iron and carbon, offering best-in-class resistance to cuts, punctures and hypodermic needles.

  • Iron and carbon threads

    Best-in-class resistance to cuts, punctures and hypodermic needles in a flexible construction.

  • Tested beyond EN 388

    ASTM F2878 forces a 25-gauge needle through the material at fixed speed, covering the sharps hazards EN 388 misses.

  • Lightweight and washable

    Breathable, recyclable and washable, so the protection survives the laundry cycle.

Type Needle resistance
Tested to ASTM F2878
Found in PowerCut
